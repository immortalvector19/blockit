# FocusShield

FocusShield is a digital wellbeing and deep work application that shields users from distractions through timed focus sessions, strict app restrictions, custom overlay reminders, and intentional daily goals.

## Language

### Focus & Sessions

**Focus Session**:
An active, bounded period of dedicated work during which blocking rules are strictly enforced and notifications are suppressed.
_Avoid_: Block cycle, lock timer, focus run

**Focus Goal**:
A specific objective or actionable milestone planned for a particular date or focus session that the user aims to complete.
_Avoid_: Task, todo item, assignment, target

**Daily Goal Plan**:
The collection of planned Focus Goals associated with a specific calendar date that automatically load into Focus Sessions occurring on that day.
_Avoid_: Agenda, calendar schedule, daily todo list

**Session Strictness**:
The level of enforcement applied during a Focus Session determining how difficult or impossible it is to stop early (Normal, Strict, or Locked).
_Avoid_: Hardness, severity, difficulty

**Break**:
A temporary, timed pause of app blocking during a Focus Session that freezes session countdown and prevents interruption of overall focus duration.
_Avoid_: Hall pass, timeout, unblock window

### Profiles & Enforcement

**Focus Profile**:
A named configuration defining blocking rules, app restrictions (allowlist or blocklist), and schedule boundaries.
_Avoid_: Preset, group, ruleset

**Block Overlay**:
A full-screen shield window intercepting prohibited apps during an active session, displaying motivational mantras, remaining time, break controls, and actionable Focus Goals.
_Avoid_: Blocker screen, lockscreen, barrier

**Overlay Customization**:
Personal visual assets and affirmations applied to the Block Overlay, including solid themes, single images, 4-photo moodboard collages, and motivational quotes.
_Avoid_: Skin, backdrop, theme pack
